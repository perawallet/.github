# Code of Conduct

Pera's open source projects follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't welcome in our repos, issues, PRs or community channels.

To report a problem, use our [contact page](https://perawallet.app/contact-us/) and mention "Code of Conduct". Reports are handled privately by the Pera team.
