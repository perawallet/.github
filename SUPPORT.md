# Getting help

**Wallet or account problem?** Missing funds, a transaction you don't recognise, recovery, Ledger or app issues: go to **[support.perawallet.app](https://support.perawallet.app)**. GitHub issues are public and aren't the place for account problems.

> Pera will never ask for your recovery phrase or private key. Anyone who does, including someone claiming to be Pera support, is trying to steal your funds.

**Integrating Pera into a dApp?** Check the [developer docs](https://docs.perawallet.app) and the [demo dApp](https://perawallet.github.io/pera-demo-dapp/) first. If you've found a bug in one of our SDKs, open an issue in that repo.

**Security vulnerability?** Don't open an issue. Follow our [security policy](https://github.com/perawallet/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
